Keyline is a free and open-source Android keyboard with customizable layouts, themes and background images.

Features:
• Persian, English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Russian and Turkish layouts
• Themes, glass-style keys and your own background image
• Toolbar that gives way to word suggestions while you type
• One-handed and floating modes that you can resize by dragging
• Adjustable vibration strength, key height and key opacity
• Clipboard history, emoji panel, text editing pad and translator
• No ads, no analytics and no tracking

The translator sends the text you choose to translate to the Google Translate web service. Voice typing uses the speech recognizer installed on your device.
